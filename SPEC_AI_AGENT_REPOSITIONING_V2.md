## 1. Objective

Refocus the existing Terry AI Lab website from broad AI implementation into a clear commercial offer:

> Business diagnosis first
> Tailor-made AI Agent execution second

The website must explain that Terry AI Lab does not only give advice and does not only sell tools. A business consultant first identifies the real operational or marketing problem. Terry and the team then design and build an AI Agent Workflow that handles a defined part of the work.

The first confirmed product is a customer-enquiry and follow-up AI Agent. The tutoring-centre workflow is an example, not an education-only product.

### Product boundary

The website must not imply that every process is fully autonomous. Use human confirmation for appointments, sensitive decisions, exceptions and final approval.

The tutoring-centre example demonstrates this pattern:

家長 WhatsApp 查詢 → AI 回覆及收集資料 → AI 整理新 Lead → AI 查詢可用時間 → Staff 確認 → 建立試堂 → AI 提醒 Follow-up → 更新記錄
